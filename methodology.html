Methodology

At Data in DP, transparency is a core principle. Below is an explanation of how we calculate the key metrics presented across our platforms.

1. PITI Monthly Payment Estimate

The True Cost Dashboard uses a standard PITI (Principal, Interest, Taxes, Insurance) formula based on the following assumptions:

  • Down Payment: 20% of the median home price.
  • Interest Rate: Current 30-year fixed mortgage rate sourced from FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data).
  • Property Tax: Annual effective tax rate sourced from ACS data, multiplied by home price, divided by 12.
  • Insurance: Annual premium estimate sourced from HearthMap/FEMA data, divided by 12.

2. Livability Score

The Livability Score is a composite of six axes, normalized into a percentile rank (0–100) across all 3,100+ U.S. counties. The default weighting is:

  • Affordability (Price-to-Income Ratio): 25%
  • Amenities (OSM point density): 20%
  • Stability (FHFA price volatility): 15%
  • Momentum (Recent price appreciation): 15%
  • Safety (FBI crime data): 15%
  • Health (CDC PLACES indicators): 10%

3. Solar ROI & Payback Period

Calculated for a standard 6kW residential solar system:

  • Net Cost: Gross system cost minus the 30% federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC).
  • Production: Based on NREL solar radiation data (NSRDB) for the specific county.
  • Savings: Based on local EIA electricity rates, assuming a 2.5% annual utility rate escalation and 0.5% panel degradation.
  • Payback: The year when cumulative savings exceed the net system cost.

4. FEMA Climate Risk Score

The risk score shown in the dashboard is pulled directly from the FEMA National Risk Index (NRI), which combines expected annual loss from natural hazards, social vulnerability, and community resilience.
