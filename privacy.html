Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Data in DP ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible from dataindp.com and its subdomains (housing, mortgage, propertytax, insurance), the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected and recorded by Data in DP, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Log Files

Data in DP follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring and exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of this information is to analyze trends, administer the site, track users' movement on the website, and gather broad demographic information.

1.2 Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Data in DP uses cookies. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and other information. We also use localStorage and sessionStorage to remember settings such as custom score weights and to cache data for performance. You can clear these at any time through your browser settings.

1.3 Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns — for example, which pages are most visited. Google Analytics collects information such as your IP address (truncated where applicable), browser, device, and pages visited. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Cookies

2.1 Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. These cookies may include the DoubleClick DART cookie.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info. For more information on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, please see "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services".

2.2 Third-Party Ad Vendors

Third-party ad servers or ad networks may use technologies such as cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Data in DP, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit. Data in DP has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

2.3 Third-Party Privacy Policies

Data in DP's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective privacy policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. Those policies may include their practices and instructions about how to opt out of certain options. A list of these policies and their links is available at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website and its data tools.
  • To measure aggregate traffic and understand which content is useful.
  • To display advertising through Google AdSense and its partners.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

4. Data Retention

Server log data is retained for a limited period for security and diagnostic purposes and is then deleted or aggregated. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics retention settings. Settings you save in your browser — such as custom score weights — are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to us.

5. Your Rights (GDPR and CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. To exercise these rights, contact us at indp1015@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Advertising opt-out summary: You can manage or disable personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info. You can also block cookies entirely in your browser, though some site features may not work as intended.

6. Children's Information

Protecting children while using the internet is important to us. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their children's online activity. Data in DP does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child provided this kind of information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will do our best to promptly remove such information from our records.

7. External Links

This site contains links to other websites, including our own subdomains and public data sources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at indp1015@gmail.com or via our Contact page.
