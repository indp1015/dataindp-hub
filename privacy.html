Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Data in DP ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible from dataindp.com and its subdomains (housing, mortgage, propertytax, insurance, solar), the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Log Files

Data in DP follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information. The purpose is to analyze trends, administer the site, and gather broad demographic information.

1.2 Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Data in DP uses cookies. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and other information. We also use localStorage to remember custom score weights on the Livability page.

2. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons that are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Data in DP has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns. Google Analytics collects information such as your IP address (truncated where applicable), browser, device, and pages visited. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Your Rights (GDPR and CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. To exercise these rights, contact us at indp1015@gmail.com.

Advertising opt-out summary: You can manage or disable personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info.

5. Children's Information

Data in DP does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child provided this kind of information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will do our best to promptly remove such information from our records.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at indp1015@gmail.com or via our Contact page.
