How to Use a PITI Calculator: A Complete Guide

When shopping for a home, it is tempting to focus only on the principal and interest of your mortgage. But smart buyers know that the true cost of a home is captured by PITI: Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance.

1. What is PITI?

PITI is the acronym lenders use to describe the four components of a standard monthly mortgage payment:

  • Principal: The amount you borrow to buy the home.
  • Interest: The lender's fee for loaning you the money (based on your rate).
  • Taxes: The local property taxes collected by your county or municipality.
  • Insurance: The homeowners insurance premium required to protect the property.

2. Why Taxes and Insurance Matter So Much

Many first-time buyers make the mistake of budgeting only for principal and interest. However, in some counties, property taxes and insurance can add 50% or more to your monthly payment. For example, a $400,000 home in a county with a 2.2% property tax rate will have a monthly tax bill of over $730—which is often more than the interest portion of the loan.

4. Don't Forget HOA and Maintenance

While PITI covers the four main components, it does not include Homeowners Association (HOA) dues, flood insurance (if required), or ongoing maintenance (typically 1% of home value per year). When using our calculator, always leave a buffer in your budget for these extra costs.
